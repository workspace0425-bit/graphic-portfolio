CONCEPT SOCIAL MEDIA & BRAND CONTENT DESIGN

Self-Initiated Concept · Visual Identity · Social-First Art Direction · Multi-Format Content System

PROJECT PREMISE

An in-depth exploration of a cohesive social media identity for a modern coffee brand — orchestrating warm neutral photography, refined typography, product storytelling, and everyday lifestyle content into a unified visual world.

01 // ECOSYSTEM

THE SOCIAL SYSTEM

The complete Instagram profile and feed architecture engineered to establish immediate brand recognition, rhythmic grid balance, and seamless visual continuity across diverse post types.

01

PROFILE IDENTITY

Consistent icon mark, editorial bio structure, and unified tone of voice from the first touchpoint.

02

FEED CONSISTENCY

Carefully paced alternation between bold packaging, lifestyle moments, and quiet product focus.

03

VISUAL HIERARCHY

Distinct scale, purposeful typography framing, and strong focal anchors across square assets.

04

RECURRING TYPOGRAPHY

Structured grotesques paired with restrained serif accents for an elevated specialty coffee feel.

05

COLOUR SYSTEM

Deep burgundy, warm cream, roasted coffee tones, and warm organic neutrals across every scene.

06

MULTI-FORMAT PRESENCE

Adaptive executions spanning packaging reveals, community storytelling, and promotional campaigns.

03 // DESIGN PILLARS

THE VISUAL LANGUAGE

A unified visual framework uniting diverse post executions into one unmistakable aesthetic:

  • Warm neutral & natural-light photography
  • Rich signature burgundy & cream palette
  • Restrained typographic scale & clean alignments
  • Editorial composition with generous whitespace
  • Relatable, authentic everyday lifestyle imagery
  • Recurring brand phrases & consistent logo placement

Different content types, one recognisable brand language.

04 // MULTI-FORMAT STRATEGY

CONTENT ADAPTABILITY

Demonstrating the capacity to fluidly adapt a single brand identity across multiple digital storytelling formats:

  • Product Hero & Packaging reveals
  • High-conversion promotional campaigns
  • Everyday workspace lifestyle integration
  • Restrained minimal art moments
  • Community-focused brand storytelling
  • Playful conceptual & experimental imagery

Built for social engagement without brand dilution.

“SAME BEANS. DIFFERENT STORIES.”

From tactile packaging close-ups to quiet morning routines and high-energy promotional campaigns, MOFFEE exemplifies how a robust visual system sustains endless narrative variation while reinforcing brand loyalty.
