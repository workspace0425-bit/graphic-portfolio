06

SOCIAL CONTENT DESIGN

STORIES + CAROUSEL · CONCEPT SYSTEMS

Self-Initiated Concepts · Format Adaptability · Story Sequencing · Multi-Slide Feed Narrative

CURATORIAL PREMISE

This project demonstrates the ability to design for fundamentally different social media formats — adapting visual communication, layout cadence, and storytelling strategies according to the specific platform mechanics and distinct brand identities of NÉVRE and SOLRA.

01 // BRAND ONE · FASHION & EDITORIAL

NÉVRE

INSTAGRAM STORY DESIGN

DESIGN APPROACH & PRINCIPLES

Designing a connected Story sequence rather than treating each frame as an isolated graphic. Built with a sleek, monochromatic editorial sensibility tailored for quick vertical consumption.

  • Narrative story pacing from teaser to product reveal
  • Mobile-first composition with safe-zone UI awareness
  • Restrained high-contrast typography hierarchy
  • Intentional interactive sticker and swipe-up CTA placement
  • Seamless visual continuity across consecutive frames
CAPABILITY

Demonstrates: Ability to create structured, narrative-driven Instagram Story content designed specifically for mobile viewing.

02 // BRAND TWO · SKINCARE & SUNCARE

SOLRA

INSTAGRAM CAROUSEL DESIGN

DESIGN APPROACH & PRINCIPLES

Engineered as a 5-slide educational and product showcase carousel designed for high retention and seamless swipe-through engagement across the main Instagram feed.

  • Carousel storytelling with seamless visual rhythm across slides
  • Clear information hierarchy balancing product benefits and science
  • Vibrant electric azure and sun-drenched photography palette
  • Editorial layout tailored for square / 4:5 feed consumption
  • Progressive narrative momentum from slide 01 hook to slide 05 payoff
CAPABILITY

Demonstrates: Ability to build a multi-slide narrative where each frame communicates independently while contributing to one larger story.

01 / 05 // COVER HOOK SUNCARE PRODUCT INTRODUCTION
02 / 05 // INGREDIENT SPOTLIGHT BOTANICAL UV DEFENCE
03 / 05 // TEXTURE & APPLICATION FEATHERLIGHT FORMULATION
04 / 05 // CLINICAL BENEFIT BARRIER REPAIR & HYDRATION
05 / 05 // CLOSING CTA & SUMMARY FINAL BRAND CALLOUT
FORMAT STRATEGY COMPARISON

FORMAT-SPECIFIC DESIGN PRINCIPLES

NÉVRE

STORY FORMAT (VERTICAL 9:16)
  • Short-form vertical narrative designed for rapid consumption
  • Mobile-first full-screen immersive composition
  • Sequential frames creating anticipation across taps
  • Interaction-focused with prominent CTA touchpoints

SOLRA

CAROUSEL FORMAT (FEED MULTI-SLIDE)
  • Multi-slide feed architecture built for dwell-time and swipe retention
  • Information-led educational & product storytelling
  • Slide-to-slide continuity with edge-bleeding elements
  • Balanced combination of hook, depth, and concluding conversion

DESIGNING FOR THE FORMAT

Effective social design is never about applying a single uniform template. It demands an acute understanding of how format ergonomics, user intent, platform mechanics, and distinct brand positioning intersect to deliver impactful creative work.
