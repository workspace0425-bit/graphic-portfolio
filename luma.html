01

LUMA

CONCEPT BRAND IDENTITY + APPAREL DESIGN

Self-Initiated Concept Project · Women's Activewear · Brand Identity & Design System

“A self-initiated fitnesswear concept built around confidence, movement, and expressive everyday style. Developed to explore how vibrant brand identity, structured packaging, and multi-channel storytelling can break category predictability in women's athletic apparel.”

CONCEPT PROJECT BRAND IDENTITY APPAREL DESIGN PACKAGING DIELINES CAMPAIGN ART DIRECTION
01 // THE IDEA

FEEL GOOD, MOVE FREE

LUMA was conceived as a women's performance wear concept around the belief that feeling energized in what you wear directly impacts how you move and show up.

02 // THE CHALLENGE

BREAKING PREDICTABILITY

Women's athletic wear is heavily crowded with minimal, muted, monochrome identities. The challenge was designing a system that feels expressive and feminine without sacrificing technical authority.

03 // THE OBJECTIVE

DISTINCTIVE BRAND SYSTEM

Build a complete, scalable brand architecture—balancing technical performance, comfort, and bold self-expression through color, silhouette, and playful graphic accents.

04 // CREATIVE DIRECTION
BRIGHT ENERGETIC CONFIDENT PLAYFUL
05 // INTENDED AUDIENCE

EMPOWERED & ACTIVE

Geared toward active women who value intentional lifestyle design. They look for activewear that transitions smoothly from high-intensity training to casual city living, rejecting sterile gym aesthetics.

06 // DESIGN RATIONALE

STRATEGIC DECISIONS

Warm terracotta and energizing coral hues were chosen to evoke warmth and empowerment. Clean modern typography ensures legibility on garments and packaging, while energetic layout rhythms fuel social engagement.
