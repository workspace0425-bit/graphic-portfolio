LUMA

CONCEPT BRAND IDENTITY & APPAREL CASE STUDY

Self-Initiated Concept Project · Women's Activewear · Brand Identity & Design System

01 // THE IDEA

FEEL GOOD, MOVE FREE

LUMA is a women's activewear brand built around a simple belief: feeling good in what you wear can change how you move.

02 // THE CHALLENGE

BREAKING PREDICTABILITY

Create an activewear identity that feels expressive, feminine and visually exciting in a category often dominated by minimal, predictable design.

03 // THE OBJECTIVE

DISTINCTIVE BRAND SYSTEM

Build a distinctive brand system that balances performance, comfort and self-expression through colour, silhouette and playful graphic details.

04 // CREATIVE DIRECTION
BRIGHT ENERGETIC CONFIDENT PLAYFUL
05 // INTENDED AUDIENCE

CONFIDENT & ACTIVE WOMEN

Designed for active women seeking workout apparel that combines technical comfort with expressive style, breaking away from standard muted fitness fashion.

06 // DESIGN RATIONALE

VIBRANCY & SCALABILITY

Warm terracotta and bold typography were chosen to convey energy and empowerment, designed as a unified system across physical apparel, packaging dielines, and digital campaigns.
