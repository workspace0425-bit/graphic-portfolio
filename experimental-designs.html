04

EXPERIMENTAL DESIGNS

A VISUAL STUDY EXPLORING DIFFERENT DESIGN LANGUAGES, COMPOSITIONS AND AESTHETICS

Self-Initiated Work · Aesthetic Adaptability · 10 Curated Explorations · Typographic Systems

CURATORIAL PREMISE
“This collection explores how visual systems can shift across different contexts, styles and moods — demonstrating adaptability rather than one fixed design language.”
01

MINIMALISM

SPATIAL RESTRAINT // ESSENTIAL FORMS
DESIGN APPROACH

A minimalist graphic and editorial composition focused on simplicity, clarity, intentional spatial tension, and essential visual elements stripped of arbitrary decoration.

Demonstrates: Ability to work with restrained visual systems, precise spatial tension, and refined negative space.

02

SWISS DESIGN

GRID DISCIPLINE // OBJECTIVE ORDER
DESIGN APPROACH

Swiss-inspired graphic design focusing on structured asymmetric composition, clean grotesque typography, rigorous grid thinking, and uncompromising visual hierarchy.

Demonstrates: Mastery of structured typographic hierarchies, modular grid architectures, and objective visual clarity.

03

BRUTALISM

RAW CONTRAST // STRUCTURAL DISRUPTION
DESIGN APPROACH

Brutalist poster and editorial design using raw, high-contrast typography, structural disruption, unpolished geometry, and an intentionally uncompromising visual language.

Demonstrates: Confidence with high-impact, anti-decorative graphic systems that command attention through raw tension.

04

NEO-BRUTALISM

DIGITAL BLOCKS // HIGH-ENERGY CONTRAST
DESIGN APPROACH

Neo-brutalist digital and editorial design combining bold saturated color blocks, retro interface components, stark black borders, and deliberately energetic information hierarchy.

Demonstrates: Ability to synthesize contemporary UI culture and vibrant contrast into compelling, playful visual narratives.

05

Y2K AESTHETICS

CYBER NOSTALGIA // CHROME TYPEFORMS
DESIGN APPROACH

Y2K-inspired poster and graphic design drawing from turn-of-the-millennium digital aesthetics, futuristic cyber typography, chrome-rendered textures, and early-web visual motifs.

Demonstrates: Versatility in translating niche subcultural trends and tactile digital materials into relevant creative directions.

06

ART DECO

GEOMETRIC SYMMETRY // ORNAMENTAL LUXURY
DESIGN APPROACH

Art Deco-inspired poster design emphasizing precise geometric ornamentation, axial symmetry, elegant display typography, and a refined decorative visual cadence.

Demonstrates: Precision in handling intricate vector ornamentation, historical design cues, and luxurious structural balance.

07

EDITORIAL DESIGN

ART DIRECTION // NARRATIVE PACING
DESIGN APPROACH

Contemporary editorial and magazine layout centered on rigorous art direction, asymmetric image composition, nuanced typographic pairing, and dynamic editorial pacing.

Demonstrates: Advanced editorial art direction, sophisticated image-text choreography, and long-form visual storytelling.

08

GLASSMORPHISM

OPTICAL TRANSLUCENCY // LAYERED DEPTH
DESIGN APPROACH

An experimental digital graphic study exploring multi-layered optical translucency, chromatic refraction, frosted glass blurring, and spatial depth perception.

Demonstrates: Deep understanding of light physics, complex multi-plane depth illusions, and modern digital surface rendering.

09

FUTURISTIC & SPECULATIVE DESIGN

INTERFACE MODULARITY // TECH WORLDBUILDING
DESIGN APPROACH

Futuristic and speculative graphic design combining technology-driven HUD interface elements, monospaced data matrices, digital typography, and forward-looking aesthetic systems.

Demonstrates: Capacity to engineer technical data visualizations, speculative design systems, and forward-looking brand worlds.

10

PSYCHEDELIC DESIGN

ORGANIC WARPING // EXPRESSIVE CHROMATICS
DESIGN APPROACH

Psychedelic experimental poster design leveraging fluid vector distortion, surreal compositional juxtaposition, undulating typography, and altered chromatic perception.

Demonstrates: Ability to break traditional layout conventions with expressive organic illustration and fearless chromatic experimentation.

“Different briefs demand different visual languages. This collection is an exploration of how far that language can shift.”

From rigorous Swiss grids and stark minimalism to expressive psychedelia and speculative UI, this archive stands as a testament to stylistic versatility, visual curiosity, and execution precision across diverse creative challenges.
